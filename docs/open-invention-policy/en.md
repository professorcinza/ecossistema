# Open Invention Policy

**High-quality inventions, patent-free, open to everyone**

*Languages:* [English](en.md) · [Português](pt.md) · [中文](zh.md)

---

## Preamble

In addition to the Unilateral Declaration of Commitment and the Open Collaboration Charter, this policy establishes the intellectual-property regime for everything the author publishes in this project's prototype series: high-quality inventions, deliberately unpatented, open to the world — with authorship always credited.

## Article 1 — The "patent-free" commitment

1. The author **will not seek patents** on the inventions he publishes in this prototype series;
2. Public, dated publication in the repository's history constitutes **prior art**: it prevents third parties from claiming an exclusive patent over the same invention in countries that require absolute novelty;
3. "Patent-free" means, here: no one — not the author, not a company, not a government — may turn what this series publishes into an exclusive monopoly.

## Article 2 — The licenses (all with mandatory attribution)

1. **Software**: GNU AGPL 3.0 or later — anyone may use, study, modify, and distribute it, keeping the work open and the author credited;
2. **Hardware**: CERN Open Hardware Licence (CERN-OHL-S) — the same spirit, for designs, circuits, and schematics;
3. **Documents and other content**: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0);
4. In special cases, another free license with mandatory attribution may be adopted, provided it is declared in the published file itself;
5. Works already published keep the license in force at their publication; a project is relicensed only through an explicit commit, never by rewriting history.

## Article 3 — Attribution

1. Anyone who uses, adapts, or redistributes a work from this series must credit: **"Cleiton Moura Loura — Brazil–China Friendship and Mutual Support project"**, with a link to the repository of origin;
2. Credit is not a courtesy: it is a condition of the license, enforceable by law;
3. The repository's public version history remains a permanent record of authorship, date, and priority.

## Article 4 — Non-exclusivity

No person, company, or nation obtains exclusive rights over any work of this series. What is published here is available to everyone on the same terms — Brazilians, Chinese, and people of any country.

## Article 5 — What stays outside this policy

1. Inventions not published in this series are not covered by this policy;
2. The author reserves the right to adopt other regimes for future works outside the project — but what has been published here as open **remains open**.
