# Layers and dimensions of energy use

**Avatar-Energy · Base document 03 · August 22, 2026**

---

## The layers — the energy stack

Like a protocol stack, each layer serves the one above:

| Layer | Name | Function | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Source** | Where it comes from | Sun, atomic nucleus, gravity, chemistry |
| 1 | **Conversion** | Making it useful | Panel, turbine, engine, metabolism |
| 2 | **Storage** | Shifting in time | Battery, reservoir, hydrogen, black-hole battery |
| 3 | **Transport** | Shifting in space | Grid, pipeline, microwave beam |
| 4 | **Application** | Final use | Motor, lamp, processor, furnace |
| 5 | **Management** | Measure, allocate, optimize the others | Sensors, algorithms, markets — **the avatar's layer** |

Layer 5 is the **meta-layer**: it moves no energy, but decides how all others move. It is where the avatar lives — and from where it sees the whole stack.

## The use dimensions

| # | Dimension | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Motion** | Moving things and people | Transport, industry, logistics |
| 2 | **Heat** | Heating and processing | Comfort, steelmaking, cooking |
| 3 | **Cold** | Cooling | Cold chain, air conditioning, cryogenics |
| 4 | **Light** | Illuminating and radiating | Lighting, sterilization, artificial photosynthesis |
| 5 | **Information** | Computing and communicating | Data centers, networks, Landauer |
| 6 | **Matter** | Transforming substances | Chemistry, cement, recycling, embodied energy |
| 7 | **Life** | Sustaining organisms | Food, health, metabolism, ecosystems |

Each dimension has its own efficiency profile and waste signature — hence dimensions, not labels: they demand distinct treatment.

## The avatar's action space

The product of the three structures defines every possible gesture:

```
ACTION = operation × layer × dimension

7 operations × 6 layers × 7 dimensions = 294 action cells
```

Example cells:

| Operation | Layer | Dimension | Concrete action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maximize | Conversion | Light | photon-to-lux luminous efficiency |
| Store | Storage | Information | data-center batteries at peak |
| Redirect | Transport | Heat | district heating from waste heat |
| Minimize waste | Application | Cold | lossless cold chain |
| Allocate | Management | Life | energy priority for hospitals in crisis |

And at Kardashev scale (base 02), the same 294 cells reappear larger: "Maximize × Source × Light" at Type II is the complete Dyson swarm.
