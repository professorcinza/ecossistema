# Concept

**Avatar-Energy — the energy agent**

*Architect's definition, August 22, 2026:*

> This project's function is to **maximize, redirect, allocate, conserve, store, distribute, and minimize waste of energy.**

---

## The seven operations

| # | Operation | Technical meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Maximize** | Raise the yield of generation and use: more useful energy per unit of resource |
| 2 | **Redirect** | Route flows: take energy from where it is surplus to where it is needed, at the right moment |
| 3 | **Allocate** | Decide destinations by priority, cost, and need — including deferring consumption (load shifting) |
| 4 | **Conserve** | Cut consumption through efficiency: do the same with less |
| 5 | **Store** | Capture surplus for future use — batteries, hydrogen, gravity, heat |
| 6 | **Distribute** | Deliver across the network to the point of use, with minimal intermediaries |
| 7 | **Minimize waste** | Cut losses across the whole chain: transmission, heat dissipation, idle consumption |

## The cycle

```
generation → storage → routing → allocation → distribution → consumption
    ↑____________________________________________________________________|
                  loss minimization at every link
```

The seven operations cover the complete energy lifecycle — from source to use, including the return of surplus.

## The Avatar

**Avatar** (Sanskrit *avatāra*, "descent", incarnation): the figure that descends into a system to act upon it. In this project, the avatar is **the agent that embodies the seven operations** — descending into the grid, the building, the body, or the data, and managing energy in its name.
