**Avatar-Energy · Base document 17 · August 22, 2026**

*Architect's decision: the ecosystem's existing projects integrate into the Teia Phone — each with a defined role. The device is the point where the webs meet.*

| Project | What it is | Role in the Teia Phone | Integration |
|---|---|---|---|

## The convergence reading

Each project was born alone and finds the others in the pocket: **the mind** (kernel), **the method** (TEIA), **the web** (rede), **the vigil** (poder-visivel), **the imagination** (inkos), **the training** (civilization) — on the **common chassis** (MOD) governed by **the house** (ponte). The Teia Phone is no longer a product of the ecosystem: **it is the ecosystem made object**.

*Method note: ECO-003 applied norm IV in its first hour — the MAL spec is born from reverse spec of teia-rede, because the living project with highest coverage becomes anchor. The constitution working on the day it was written.*
