# Avatar-Energy

**The energy agent.**

This project's function is to **maximize, redirect, allocate, conserve, store, distribute, and minimize waste of energy** — the seven operations covering the complete energy cycle, from source to use.

A project of the [ponte-brasil-china](https://github.com/professorcinza/ponte-brasil-china) ecosystem, governed by Article 6 of the [Collaboration Charter](https://github.com/professorcinza/ponte-brasil-china/blob/main/docs/open-collaboration-charter/en.md).
