# Energy at cosmic scale

**Avatar-Energy · Base document 02 · August 22, 2026**

## The ruler: the Kardashev scale (1964)

| Type | Scale | Approximate power | Dominant source |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Planetary | ~10¹⁶ W | all energy reaching the planet |
| II | Stellar | ~10²⁶ W | a star's full output |
| III | Galactic | ~10³⁶ W | billions of stars |

**Humanity today**: ~10¹³ W — approximately **Kardashev 0.73**. Each of the avatar's seven operations exists in miniature today and whole at each step of the scale.

## Stellar scale

**Dyson sphere** (Freeman Dyson, 1960) — structure around a star to capture nearly all its output. Viability note: the solid shell is **dynamically unstable** and materially implausible; Dyson's own formulation was already a **swarm** — the realizable variant.

**Dyson swarm** — millions of independent orbiting collectors beaming energy by microwave or laser to wherever it is allocated.

**Star lifting** — removing matter from the star itself, as fuel or to extend its life.

**Laser propulsion** — light sails pushed by high-power laser arrays (in the direction of Breakthrough Starshot: gram-scale probes at significant fractions of c).

## Galactic scale and beyond

**Black holes** — the most efficient sources known:
- *Accretion disk*: up to ~42% mass→energy conversion (fusion: ~0.7%);
- *Penrose process* (1969): extracting rotational energy from the ergosphere — up to ~29% of the hole's mass;
- *Superradiance*: wave amplification by a rotating hole;
- *Hawking radiation*: the final converter — small black holes as "batteries" converting matter to energy at near 100%.

**Dark energy** — hypothesis for millennia ahead, should the universe's expansion prove accessible. No known mechanism; recorded as legitimate speculative frontier.

**Transmission networks** — energy collected in space, sent wirelessly (microwave/laser) to colonies and planets. Demonstrated at small scale (terrestrial microwave beaming in 1975; in space by Caltech's SSPD-1, 2023).

## The seven operations at Kardashev scale

| Operation | Today (K ~0.73) | Scale II–III |
|---|---|---|
| **Maximize** | panel and turbine efficiency | full stellar-output capture |
| **Redirect** | grid routing | microwave beams between orbits and worlds |
| **Allocate** | load priority, peak hours | stellar energy among colonies and fleets |
| **Conserve** | building and motor efficiency | photon drives with minimal loss |
| **Store** | batteries, hydrogen | black-hole batteries; stars as reservoirs |
| **Distribute** | transmission lines | interplanetary beam networks |
| **Minimize waste** | transmission losses, idle heat | thermodynamic radiators — the final limit: all excess becomes heat to radiate |

**The thermodynamic limit**: at any scale the Second Law collects its toll — waste heat must be radiated to space. It is precisely this infrared signature, incidentally, that would let us detect alien Dyson swarms: **waste is the visible part of any capture**.
