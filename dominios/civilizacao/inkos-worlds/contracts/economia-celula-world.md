World contract — ECONOMIA DA CÉLULA (play world v1)
Derived from: hub contracts ECONOMIA-CELULA.md, MERENDA-SPEC.md, SAVE-OFICIOS.md, SIMULADOR-OFICIO-BR.md. The hub's economic doctrine as a PLAYABLE microsociety.

PREMISE
A Brazilian municipality neighborhood (ABC paulista). The doctrine: trabalho é canalização de energia para um fim — produce what is missing, stop the noise. The player organizes a MICRO-ECONOMY of real occupations, proving that if everyone organizes, everyone thrives. Opposition is not a villain: it is entropy, seasonality, and the siren song of the easy scam.

TIME FLOW
1 step ≈ 1 week of neighborhood time. Seasonal calendar drives demand (school year, festivities, winter, rainy season).

CORE MECHANICS
- 7 OFFICES (ofícios) as playable archetypes: hacker de bairro (tech repair/connectivity), motoboy logístico, feirante, catador de materiais, técnico de manutenção, merendeira, zelador. Each has income curve, physical toll, and social capital curve.
- MERENDA-SPEC (the flagship): school-feeding hub. SPEC 2 law (canon): eating habits form in childhood — the merenda is infrastructure, not charity. KPIs: meals served, local-producer share, food waste, parent participation.
- ECONOMY LOOP: energy in → skill → product/service → trust network → energy recycled. Trust is the real currency (REDE); money ($) is a lagging indicator.
- SCAM VECTOR: at every hardship spike, one "easy money" alternative appears (pyramid, diverted donations, betting affiliate). Taking it boosts $ short-term, permanently corrodes REDE — and the simulation shows the neighborhood's memory.

CHARACTER AGENTS
- ANCIÃ DA COORDENAÇÃO: coordinator elder; wants succession; knows everyone's debts; hides her own burnout.
- COMMUNITY BUILDER: young organizer; wants scale; knows social media; hides that metrics inflame him.
- A FORNECEDORA (keyboard supplier, canon bilateral partner): honest supplier testing whether the cell pays on time; her credit line is the economy's shock absorber.
- AUDITORA ITINERANTE: external auditor (canon: public audit sessions); arrives unannounced; her report is the game's conscience.

ITEMS / EVIDENCE / RELATIONS
- Inventory: tools of each office, merenda stock (perishable! spoilage is real), donated equipment pending audit.
- Evidence: receipts, audit ledgers, the AUDIT SESSION as collectible artifact (canon: audit = public sessions).
- Relations: each family served, each producer, each volunteer tracked by (need / contribution / trust).

PLAYER IDENTITY
The ORGANIZER: no special powers beyond seeing the whole board. Delegates, never does everything (canon: "delega à equipe, assina embaixo").

VISUAL RULES
Market-morning light, ledger tables, ASCII price boards. Warm palette with graphite structure.

INTERACTION RULES
Guided choices + free actions. Consequences proportional, never moralistic: the simulation shows, the player concludes. Multi-channel inclusion rule (canon): solutions that exclude a channel of participation must visibly lose value in the simulation.
