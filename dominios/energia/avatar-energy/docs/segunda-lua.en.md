## The starting point: we are already the better moon

| Body | Albedo | Relative brightness |
|---|---|---|
| Moon | ~0.12 | 1× (full-Moon reference on Earth) |
| **Earth** | **~0.31** | **~40×** (full Earth seen from the Moon) |

Earth is the pair's superior reflector — clouds, ice and oceans beat the Moon's dark basalt. "Earthshine" is the Moon receiving Earth-as-moon. **Turning Earth into a second moon = deliberately increasing the reflection that is already the neighborhood's largest.**

## The real precedents

- **Znamya-2 (Russia, 1993)**: a 20 m orbital mirror reflected a ~5 km-wide beam onto nighttime Europe — history's first artificial glint, faint but measured;
- **Chengdu's "artificial moon" proposal (China, 2018)**: a mirror satellite to illuminate the city at night at a claimed brightness up to 8× the Moon — never flew, but the physics was debated seriously;
- **The lesson of both**: reflecting sunlight into the night is **engineering known in miniature** — what's missing is scale and will, not physics.

## What the real Second Moon would be

The operation mobilized is **redirect** — base 14's lever #2, at planetary scale:

```
swarm of orbital reflectors (thin, passive)
```

**The energetic elegance**: reflectors are **passive** — zero combustion, zero conversion, pure geometry. Free photons redirected: the *redirect* operation in absolute purity, the same gesture as a Dyson mirror (base 02), aimed inward.

## The three honesties

1. **Climate**: touching the planetary radiation balance is geoengineering — the mirror that illuminates also cools (or heats, if misaimed); no beam orbits without serious climate modeling behind it (CIV-ENE-006: the thermal budget is the absolute limit);
2. **Ecology**: night is habitat — insect cycles, migratory birds, corals spawning in darkness. Illuminating the global night carries biological cost; the Second Moon would have to know **how to extinguish itself** where night is life;
3. **Governance**: who aims the mirror, lights whom? A global reflector is global power — which is why this base only makes sense under GOV-001 (neutral foundation) and never under a single flag.

## The avatar's reading

The Second Moon is the *redirect* operation raised to its purest degree: **no joule created, only path changed** — and Earth, which has always been the better reflector, assuming the role on purpose. Between Chengdu's proposal and a global swarm lies the same distance as between a toy rocket and an orbital one: scale, not miracle.
