# The frontier: the cutting edge that doesn't exist — yet

**Avatar-Energy · Base document 08 · August 22, 2026**

*The method's turn: from reverse-engineering what exists (bases 05–07) to forward-engineering what is being born. Every entry dated and staged: **🔬 research** · 🛫 pilot · 🏭 commercial edge.*

---

## Frontier rules

1. Every claim has date and source;
2. Market optimism is not a result — distinguish technical milestone from promise;
3. Legitimate speculation, but labeled;
4. Map each technology to its layer (base 03) and the operations it empowers.

## Layer 0 · Source

**Nuclear fusion** 🛫 — Helion: 150 million °C D-T temperature record (Feb/2026); net gain **not yet announced** on Polaris; Microsoft 2028 deadline under pressure. SPARC/CFS: target Q>10 operating in **2027**; ARC plant early 2030s. Hot capital: Inertia's US$ 450M Series A (Feb/2026). 2026 is called "the year of ignition" — **by optimists**. No one has confirmed net electricity as of this date.

**Perovskite and tandem solar** 🛫 — perovskite/silicon tandems at ~34% lab record; pilot lines in progress. Ordinary solar is already the cheapest energy in history; tandems raise the ceiling.


**Transparent / invisible solar** 🛫
- Danish record: **fully transparent cell at 12.3% efficiency with ~30% visible transparency** (2025) — fully clear stood at 1–5%, semi-transparent at 7–12%; opaque reference: ~23%; ARENA/Australia milestone: **2 m² transparent = 1 standard rooftop panel**; Korea: "invisible PV" ~10× early prototypes. What it unlocks: **BIPV** — facade glass becomes generation; the existing building stock, a power plant. Physics honesty: transparency and efficiency compete for the same photons — the record is precisely the unprecedented balance of both.
**Enhanced geothermal (EGS)** 🛫 — fracturing for geothermal anywhere: firm 24/7 carbon-free energy.

**Betavoltaics** 🔬 — nuclear-decay cells for decades-long micro-devices; niches first (sensors, implants).

## Layer 1 · Conversion

**Thermophotovoltaics (TPV)** 🛫 — cells converting radiant heat; MIT demonstrated **>40% efficiency** — turbines without moving parts.

**Green hydrogen** 🏭 — falling electrolysis costs; stationary conversion's commercial edge.

## Layer 2 · Storage (2026's hottest frontier)

**Sodium-ion** 🏭 — **the turn already happened**: CATL announced mass production 2026-01-22; **9 GWh shipped in 2025** (+150% YoY), cells at **175 Wh/kg**; GM running a grid-sodium program (Jun/2026). No lithium, no cobalt, extreme-condition safe — the chemistry of energy sovereignty.

**Sodium solid-state** 🔬 — the confluence of the two nexts. If it scales, storage democratizes entirely.

**Lithium solid-state** 🛫 — pilot production 2027–28.

**Iron-air** 🛫 — 100-hour storage at near-zero material cost; the candidate to kill seasonality.

**Quantum batteries** 🔬 — microcavity effects allowing faster collective charging; physics confirmed microscopically, engineering nonexistent.

## Layer 3 · Transport

**HVDC and HTS superconductors** 🏭/🛫 — intercontinental DC corridors; superconducting wire leaving the lab.

**Power beaming** 🛫 — microwave and laser over distance; orbital demos already happened (Caltech SSPD-1, 2023).

## Layer 4 · Application

**Space solar power (SSP)** 🛫 — collection without night or atmosphere; ESA Solaris studies; economics not yet closed.

## Layer 5 · Management — the avatar's own frontier

**Neuromorphic computing** 🛫 — spiking chips for AI inference at 10–100× less energy per operation.
**Reversible computing and the Landauer limit** 🔬 — the thermodynamic floor of computation.
**Photonic and in-memory analog computing** 🛫 — matrices computed with light or in memory itself.
