## The chain

An **energy chain** is the sequence of transformation links between source and useful use. The canonical model has three stages:

```
PRIMARY ENERGY → FINAL ENERGY → USEFUL ENERGY
 (as it exists     (reaching the     (actually doing
  in nature)        consumer)         the work)
```

Example: oil in the well → gasoline at the pump → the car's motion. Losses at every arrow.

### The multiplication law

A chain's efficiency is the **product** of its links' efficiencies:

```
η(chain) = η₁ × η₂ × … × ηₙ
```

Three consequences:
1. **No link compensates another** — a chain is as strong as its weakest link;
2. **Short chains win** — every removed link multiplies total efficiency;
3. **Shortening chains is conserving** — the *conserve* operation often acts by removing links.

### Chain examples

| Chain | Links | Approx. η |
|---|---|---|
| Diesel → engine → motion | extraction, refining, transport, combustion | ~20–30% |
| Sun → panel → battery → motor → motion | PV, charge, discharge, traction | ~15–20% |
| Sun → panel → LED → light | two links, direct conversion | among the most efficient in the useful world |
| Hydro → grid → heat pump → heat | gravity→rotation→electricity→pump (COP 3–4) | a chain that multiplies: delivers 3–4× what it consumes |

The last line teaches: some links don't merely conserve — they **amplify** (heat pumps move more heat than the energy they consume, because they transport rather than generate).

## The interconnection

**Interconnecting** is connecting one chain's output — including its residues — to another's input:

- **Cogeneration**: the power plant's waste heat warms the city;
- **Industrial symbiosis**: one factory's residue is the neighbor's feedstock;
- **Power-to-X**: surplus electricity → hydrogen → synthetic fuel → motion;
- **Thermal cascade**: high-temperature steam serves the furnace, medium serves industry, low serves district heating — the same energy, used in steps until exhausted.

Interconnecting is *minimize waste* made concrete: what would be loss in one link becomes input in another.

## From chain to web

```
chain (linear) → tree (branching) → graph (network) → cycle (closed loop)
```

Interconnection's supreme stage is the **closed cycle**: output returns to input and nothing is lost — the circular economy applied to energy. Chains that interconnect form a **web** — which is what the ecosystem's own root is called: *Teia*.
