# Fundamentals: energy

**Avatar-Energy · Base document 01 · August 22, 2026**

## Definition

**Energy** is the scalar physical quantity measuring a system's **capacity to perform work — that is, to produce transformation**. Three properties define it:

1. **Conservation** (First Law of Thermodynamics): energy is neither created nor destroyed, only transformed; every transformation degrades part of it into unusable heat (entropy, Second Law);
2. **Transformability**: every form can in principle convert into another. SI unit: joule (J);
3. **Mass equivalence**: E = mc² — mass is a form of energy.

**Etymology**: Greek *enérgeia* (ἐνέργεια), from Aristotle — "activity, work in action" (*en* "in" + *érgon* "work"). In the original sense: **the state of that which is at work**.

## Physical forms

| Form | What it is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Kinetic | Energy of motion | wind, bullet, river |
| Gravitational potential | Position in a gravitational field | reservoir water |
| Elastic potential | Deformation | spring, bow |
| Mechanical | Kinetic + potential of the system | pendulum |
| Thermal | Particle agitation | fire, steam |
| Electrical | Charge flow/potential | current, lightning |
| Magnetic | Magnetic fields | magnet, coil |
| Radiant (electromagnetic) | EM waves (light, radio, X, gamma) | sun, Wi-Fi |
| Chemical | Molecular bonds | fuel, battery |
| Nuclear | Atomic nucleus binding | fission, fusion |
| Sound | Mechanical wave in a medium | sound, ultrasound |
| Mass | Mass-energy equivalence | annihilation |
| Dark energy | Hypothetical cosmological component (~68% of the universe) | accelerated expansion |

## Current energy matrix sources

**Non-renewable**: coal · petroleum and derivatives · natural gas · nuclear fission (uranium; thorium in development).

**Renewables**: hydroelectric · wind (onshore/offshore) · solar (PV and thermal) · biomass (wood, ethanol, biodiesel, biogas) · geothermal · tidal · wave · osmotic (blue energy).

**Vectors** (store/transport, not sources): hydrogen (green/pink/blue) · batteries · compressed air · flywheels.

**Frontier**: nuclear fusion (NIF ignition 2022; ITER) · green hydrogen at scale · space solar (SSP) · ambient energy harvesting (vibration, waste heat, RF).

## Uses of the concept beyond physics

- **Biological**: ATP, metabolism, trophic chains;
- **Informational**: Landauer's principle (erasing 1 bit dissipates ≥ kT·ln2);
- **Social/economic**: human/animal labor; metaphor for capacity to act — outside physics, of another nature.

**Brazil note**: one of the most renewable matrices among major economies — hydro dominant, wind expanding, sugarcane biomass, pre-salt as the fossil counterpoint.
